## Adding yourself

When your first contribution is merged, add a row here and the same entry to
[`app/src/main/res/raw/contributors.json`](app/src/main/res/raw/contributors.json), which the app shows
under Settings → About → Contributors:

```json
{ "name": "Your Name", "role": "What you worked on", "link": "https://your.site or null" }
```
